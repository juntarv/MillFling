# Mill Fling — App Store Listing

## App Name
Mill Fling

## Subtitle
Windmill seed-sowing arcade

## Description
Mill Fling is a bright one-thumb arcade game about a folk windmill and a field waiting to be sown. Let a seed pod fly off the turning sail, burst it in mid-air, and watch the seeds scatter across a blue-and-gold countryside stitched like a village-fair embroidery.

Tap once to release the pod: early throws fly high and short, late throws fly flat and far. Tap again to burst it into a fan of seeds, and the later you burst, the tighter the spread. Wind pushes every seed sideways, so read the vane, then sow the plot quota before the pods run out while dodging ponds, stone walls, roads, hay carts and hungry crows. Work through 72 hand-built fields across eight regions, from Meadow Rise to Harvest Crown, and earn up to three stars on each to open the next region.

Choose from 24 seed varieties with their own mass, spread and drift, each with a page that shows its flight profile. Come back for Daily Sowing, a fresh field every day with a day streak to keep. Pin 40 ribbons on the Ribbon Wall, climb 20 farmer ranks with the bushels you harvest, and unlock 10 mill paints that color your sails in every run. The Farm Book keeps a ledger of every run, with harvest charts, region records and a field guide to every plot, hazard and skill.

Mill Fling is made to be picked up for a minute or for a whole evening. There are no timers to read and no text to parse mid-flight, just timing, wind and a good eye. Everything works offline and stays on your device: no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases and no network connection. Haptics and animations can each be switched off in Settings.

Turn the sails, fling the first pod and sow the meadow. The field is waiting.
